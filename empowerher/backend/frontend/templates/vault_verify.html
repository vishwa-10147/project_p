An entrepreneur shared a zero-knowledge proof with you. Paste it below to check that they registered the idea on EmpowerHer and know its contents, without the idea being revealed to you.

How it works

When the idea was registered, EmpowerHer stored a Pedersen commitment C = gᵐ·hʳ mod p, where m is a hash of the idea and r comes from a secret only the owner holds. The proof is a Schnorr-style (Okamoto) proof of knowledge of m and r, bound to the context text. Verifying it shows the owner knows the idea behind C, and reveals nothing else about it.
