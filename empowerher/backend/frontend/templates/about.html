About EmpowerHer

EmpowerHer is a privacy-preserving AI framework designed to support women entrepreneurs throughout their entrepreneurial journey.

Our Vision

To create a secure and intelligent digital platform where women can confidently develop business ideas, access opportunities and make informed entrepreneurial decisions.

What EmpowerHer Provides

EmpowerHer combines artificial intelligence with privacy-preserving technologies to provide business idea validation, government scheme recommendations, funding assistance, local market insights, document verification and intelligent guidance.

Privacy First

Sensitive entrepreneur information is protected using privacy-preserving approaches such as Homomorphic Encryption and Zero-Knowledge Proofs, helping users share necessary information without unnecessarily exposing confidential data.
