Document Verification 📄

Upload your PAN, Aadhaar, GST or Udyam certificate. EmpowerHer reads it with OCR, checks the numbers' formats and checksums, and stores the file encrypted. Only masked numbers are kept.

Verify with DigiLocker 🇮🇳

Documents in DigiLocker are issued directly by the government department, so they are genuine. With your consent we read the list of documents issued to you and mark a matching upload (for example your PAN) as verified by DigiLocker. We keep only masked numbers, never your DigiLocker login.
